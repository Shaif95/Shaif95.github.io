Looking for a PhD Program? Start With These Sites

October 19, 2026

A large share of the visitors to this site are students looking for PhD programs, so here are the sites I usually point them to first.

  • CSRankings ranks computer science programs by what their faculty actually publish.
  • PhD Stipend lets you compare stipend offers across programs before you fall in love with one.
  • Yocket helps you shortlist universities and see where students with similar profiles applied.
  • Application Review helps with reviewing application materials, including the statement of purpose.
  • FindAPhD is a searchable database of funded PhD projects, especially strong for the UK and Europe.

Use the data to build a shortlist first. Then read the recent papers of the professors you hope to work with, and write to them like a future colleague.
